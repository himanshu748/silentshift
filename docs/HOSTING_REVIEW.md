# Online demo decision

Checked November 4, 2026 against the licence page used for the downloaded EAP kit. **No public deployment was made and the two-point online-demo bonus is Not claimed.** The landing page and interactive illustration are local product UI, not a hosted runtime or a substitute for one.

The [IRIS Early Access Program Evaluation/Testing License Agreement](https://evaluation.intersystems.com/Eval/early-access/tos), section 2, limits the licence to internal testing/evaluation and says it does not permit production, development or demonstration use. Section 6 restricts disclosure of the licensed software and related information. A public replay app would still run that licensed IRIS/AI Hub software; removing fresh inference does not remove the runtime licensing question.

The [External Repository Terms of Use](https://www.intersystems.com/IERTU/) permit hosting the content they cover, but explicitly exclude content governed by separate terms. That general permission cannot establish public-hosting rights for this separately downloaded EAP kit. The [AI Hub EAP README](https://github.com/intersystems-community/ai-hub-eap/) also cautions against production use; it does not supply an exception to this agreement for a public demo.

This is a record of the terms displayed and the deployment decision, not a claim that all Community editions forbid hosting. Written vendor/organizer confirmation of the applicable licence or a separately licensed eligible runtime would be needed before reconsidering. No host was created, no card was added and no money was spent. Free-host selection was stopped at this licence gate.
